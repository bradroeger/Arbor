## Overview

This floor of the Living Pillar was once a grand magical aquarium: a sprawling research and exhibition complex devoted to aquatic life from distant realms. An unstable portal to the Plane of Water which was used to continually supply the floor with fresh water leaked something in that should have been held back. The overall idea of the aquarium is guests would imbibe a potion to allow them to breathe water and the could as the fish do glide through to experience their world in a one of a kind exhibit. Of course like many things it was not maintained beyond the purpose of impressing a few guests and dignitaries.

Now the floor is collapsing into ruin.

The remaining inhabitants consist of:

- abandoned aquatic monstrosities,
    
- mutated sea life,
    
- flooded ecosystems,
    
- and a single reclusive witch who has survived here for years.
    

But the true terror of this floor is **The Duchess** — an ancient abyssal predator from beyond the portal.

Unable to escape the Living Pillar, she has spent decades adapting to the ruins and learning every inch of the facility. She now treats the floor as her personal hunting ground. Feasting on anyone or anything that comes into range.

The moment the party enters, they become prey.

# Core Gameplay Loop

This floor is fundamentally a **survival chase dungeon** rather than a traditional room-clearing dungeon.

The players are trying to:

1. Explore enough to survive and gather resources,
    
2. Avoid or delay the Duchess,
    
3. Navigate environmental hazards,
    
4. Reach the exit before the hunt becomes overwhelming.
    

The dungeon should feel increasingly desperate over time.

# The Duchess

## Concept

The Duchess is not simply a monster encounter.

She is:

- a stalking presence,
    
- a moving environmental threat,
    
- and the source of constant tension.
    

The goal is not initially to defeat her.

The goal is to survive her at first.

The final goal is to get the Key from her belly, or disable the exit and leave that way.

# The Hunt Clock

When the players enter the floor, begin an **8-segment Hunt Clock**.

Advance the clock whenever players:

- spend significant time in a room,
    
- rest,
    
- investigate extensively,
    
- trigger loud events,
    
- or move between major areas.
    

When the clock fills:

1. Roll on the @UUID[RollTable.dbflcgdJaxeX0qY0]{Duchess Event Table},
    
2. Resolve the event,
    
3. Reset the clock with **2 fewer segments**.
    

Example progression:

- 8 segments
    
- 6 segments
    
- 4 segments
    
- 2 segments
    
- 0 segments
    

Once the clock reaches 0:

- the Duchess abandons subtlety,
    
- and begins directly assaulting the party.
    

  
When the clock shrinks things in the dungeon change, each time alter the flora or fauna. Show more collaposed or destroyed areas and add in smoke and fog.

# Duchess Tactics

## Personality

The Duchess is:

- ancient,
    
- patient,
    
- intelligent,
    
- cruel,
    
- and deeply lonely.
    

She does not merely kill.  
She hunts emotionally.

She enjoys:

- isolating prey,
    
- causing panic,
    
- forcing difficult choices,
    
- corrupting survivors.
    

---

## Combat/Encounter Tactics

- Pull isolated players into flooded areas.
    
- Attack from darkness and retreat.
    
- Use telekinesis to:
    
    - collapse structures,
        
    - separate the party,
        
    - trigger hazards.
        
- Destroy escape routes if threatened.
    
- Manipulate water levels.
    
- Force players to move before they are ready.
    

If enraged:

- she stops stalking,
    
- and becomes a catastrophic force of destruction.
    

# The Environment

## Dungeon Tone

The aquarium should feel:

- flooded,
    
- echoing,
    
- rusting,
    
- unstable,
    
- overgrown,
    
- and partially alive.
    

Think:

- shattered glass tunnels,
    
- enormous empty tanks,
    
- drowned exhibits,
    
- flickering magical lights,
    
- distant whale-like sounds.
    

---

# Environmental Rules

## Flooding

Most areas are partially submerged.

Use:

- swimming rules,
    
- difficult terrain,
    
- underwater visibility penalties,
    
- oxygen concerns where appropriate.
    

Water should alter combat constantly.

# The Witch

She provides:

- temporary safety,
    
- exposition,
    
- bargaining,
    
- and moral ambiguity.
    

Her room acts as a temporary sanctuary because:

- the Duchess cannot immediately enter,
    
- or refuses to.
    

But safety does not last forever

# Fighting Back

They players may elect to do one of the following in an appropriate room:

- Seal the doors - this is a temporary solution.
    
- Invert the pumps to drain the water - This will work but will take time
    
- bait the Duchess elsewhere this one is tricky because she can read minds.
    
- Desroy stryctyres in the floor to trap her in areas - buys time.
    
- Set traps - buys time.
