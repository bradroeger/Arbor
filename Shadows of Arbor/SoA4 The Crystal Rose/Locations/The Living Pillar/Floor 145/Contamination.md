# Main points

- While within floor 145 make periodic secret **CON saves** against the contamination present in this floor **DC 18**
    
- If a player fails by 5 or more they advance 1 contamination stages
    
- If they fail by 10 or emore they advance 2 contamination stages
    
- If they simply fail they feel awful in this place and take 1d6 poison damage.
    

## Timing

Every time the clock moves make a check.
