# Room 5 - South Walkway

---

## Summary

- A transition walkway between the freshwater and Arctic exhibits.

---

## Read-Aloud Text

- _On one side of the hallway is beautiful enclosure of freshwater fish, a fallen stone statue covered in algae and a large number of small fish occupy this space. On the other side is a winter scene complete with igloo. Large strange looking blubberous fish swim slowly through the icy looking waters and you can feel the freezing touch of the cold from the water beyond permeating through the glass. At the end of the hall is a large steel door partially opened._

---

## Atmosphere

- Sounds: Muted water movement, pipework, and damaged walkways groaning in the distance.
- Smells: Fresh water, frost, and damp metal.
- Lighting: Reflected aquatic light, colder on the Arctic side.

---

## GM Notes

- This is a transition area merely there to provide a point in time for the players.

---

## What Changes Here

- None.

## Checks

- Tinkers Tools 25: examining the pipes you realise they are transferring thermal states between the two enclosures. Ordinarily they would both be at an equilibrium but some effect is causing a refrigeration effect and expelling that heat to the other.

- Arcana 25: a close examination of the pipe on the artic side reveals a powerful magical seal constantly chilling the area.

- Perception 30: In the distance you can see that part of the landscape has been destroyed on the artic enclosure and beyond them the walkway between seem to be severely damaged.
