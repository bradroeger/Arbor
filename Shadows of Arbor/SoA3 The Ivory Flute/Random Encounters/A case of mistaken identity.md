## Read-Aloud Text

> As you traverse the well maintained cobblestone streets of the Canopy you find yourself crossing a rather busy part of the city. A cross roads is in front of you and people are coming from every direction. Most Dressed in servants garb though you wouldn't know it from the quality or craftsmanship, everything up here appears to be of a better quality after all. Still as you make your way through the crows you feel a hand on your arm, "My dear what are you doing out of school so early?"

Give the players a moment to decide what to do at this juncture. If they simply turn to look you can continue. If they panic and leave then raise the alert level of the area by 1 as [[Jules Pankhurst]] and [[Drusilla Pankhurst]] believe someone is kidnapping their grandchild.

When the players arrive at the ice cream cart:
> You're led around more than a dozen corners and zig-zag streets. Not quickly mind you but the steps are with purpose as you hold the wrinkled hand of [[Jules Pankhurst]]. "Not much further now just a few... wait how many turns was that dear?" she answers quickly "9 Lefts, 4 rights and that little squiggle bit." "Ahh yes one more left then." as he turns to the right and you find yourself in a small city square. Situated in the centre is a large multi coloured parasol. Children and their parents are gathered around it blocking much of the view but a moment later you see a blue feathered hand and 5 large brass tubs with signs underneath written in Arborian Common and Avian on a small cart. The tubs are covered in a thin layer of frost on the outside, and are Red, Yellow, Green, Blue and Purple. The 4 signs say Berry Bliss, Gingersnap Delight, Pistashio Heaven, Skyshimmer Sorbet, and Plum Midnight. Standing behind the tubs is Gil sporting a rather fine set of new clothing appropriate for the clientele he is busy serving.

The players will encounter Jules and Drusilla. Who have both mistaken them for one of their granddaughters or grandsons. Pick one of the characters at random to be the mistaken one. Jules and Drusilla are quite old and it's even difficult to know what species they truly are at this point as much of their facial features are hidden by wrinkles. Both Jules and Drusilla should not be out alone as they are mostly blind and a bit deaf but are sweet old dears and something of local celebrities at this point. 

The players may take many tacks lying or trying to convince the pair they are mistaken, which the Pankhursts will have none of of course. If the players go along with it for a while they will suggest going to get some of that new ice cream. They hear that the beastkin who makes it is a friendly sort of chap and the Pankhurts have always been very invested in making sure that beastkin efforts and ideas were expressed in the city. 

They will get wistful and the Pankhursts are good people having funneled money to the beastkins for decades in order to help their plight though they have been unable to stop the takeover of the Beastkin territory in the Garden wall. 

If the players go with them they will run into Gil who is selling his wares in the Canopy now under special allowance from the Pankhurts who employ him to do so. He may recognise the players if they interacted with them and it may get complicated very quickly as Gil loves and owes the Pankhurts much. If he thinks the players are taking advantage of them he will not hesitate to get a Silver Cloak but he does not want the Pankhursts hurt so will avoid it if he thinks the Pankhursts might get hurt in the altercation. He does know though that this is not the first time this has happened and if he thinks the players are good hearts and more importantly they did as much as they could for the Red Wings despite their problems he is more inclined to help.

Gil does know a few things and can provide a place to lie low if they need and in the worst possible situation could even smuggle them out should the need arise.

#### Mini - Dramatis Personae
[[Jules Pankhurst]] - an elderly gentlemen of quite some age, he looks human enough but it's hard to say, his wrinkles have wrinkles and it's just as likely he could be some form of [[beastkin]], [[goblinoid]] or even one of the [[touched]]. Regardless he is a kind man having lived a life of relative wealth and spent much of it giving back especially to the Red Wings and the various beastkin tribes. He is mostly blind and quite hard of hearing but he continues to do good.

[[Drusilla Pankhurst]] - an elderly woman though don't let her know that. Like her husband it's hard to tell what species she once was the ravages of time have left much of any markers behind. Not that it matters but she is not quite as far gone as her husband and does recognise when he's made an error but she's also an excellent judge of character so would never let him make a mistake that got him hurt.

The Pankhursts - They are not one of the old families but are still wealthy enough to have made a home in the Canopy. Their business is in the refining of jewels and gems for use in many different industries and magic across Arbor.

[[Gil]] - A simple ice cream merchant from the Nest. He's met the party once before or may have done. He is also a contact for the Red Wings but he's up in the Canopy at the special request of the Pankhursts who arrange for him to sell his ice creams to the local children. The Pankhursts also provide a large amount of secret funding through Gil back to the Nest to try and right the many wrongs. For this Gil is eternally grateful.

## Outcomes

### Success

- If the players manage not to arouse suspicion and be good people throughout then Gil will reward them with the following:
	- 1 piece of useful information about the Canopy
	- An escape route should they still need it
	- Free ice cream
	- Red Wing Faction Up 1

### Failure or Escape

- There are a few tense moments which could result in combat you can pull Silver Cloaks, Arcanum members or Fallen Leaves members as appropriate.

### Consequences

If the players fail to keep the peace and not arouse suspicion or bolt at the initial meeting
- **Immediate:** Raise the Alarm level by 1
- **Later:** Gil will not trust the players
- **Faction / clock change:** Red Wing Faction Down 1, Silver Cloak Faction down 1

## GM Running Tips

- **Pacing note:** Go slow the two elderly people are dottering and sweet play this up over and over again. They however are exceptionally wise and almost seem to know what the players are doing but it's never 100% sure of this. If a character is particularly vulnerable to parental love then lean into this character.
- **Sensory detail to reuse:** The smell of ice cream and treats
- **One memorable beat:** The moment the players and the Pankhursts arrive at Gils show should be both tense for a moment as Gil recogonises the players and joyful as the children present recogonise the Pankhursts and thank them for the ice cream.
- **End on:** The Pankhursts eating ice cream on the bench together surrounded by their "Grandchildren" waving the party off.
