# A case of mistaken identity

## Description

_An elderly couple mistakes one of the party for their grandchild. Playing along may lead to an unexpected treat and a glimpse of the Canopy's kinder side, while mishandling the situation could attract unwanted attention._

## GM Notes

- **Purpose:** Provide a moment of levity, introduce a benevolent and influential Canopy household, and reinforce the party's connection to the Beastkin communities.
    
- **Starting distance / positions:** The party is moving through a crowded crossroads. Jules and Drusilla approach from within the crowd.
    
- **What is happening:** The Pankhursts have mistaken a randomly chosen character for one of their grandchildren and intend to take them for ice cream.
    

The Pankhursts are genuinely kind people. They have spent decades directing money towards Beastkin communities and supporting their efforts to establish themselves in Arbor. Despite their wealth and influence, they have been unable to prevent the takeover of Beastkin territory within the Garden Wall.

They are not members of the [[Old Families]], though their gem-refining business has made them wealthy enough to maintain a home in the Canopy.

The Pankhursts have arranged special permission for Gil, an ice cream merchant from the Nest, to sell his wares in the Canopy. They also use him to channel discreet financial support back to the Nest.

The couple are well-known local celebrities. Many children recognise them and are delighted to see them.

## Trigger and Setup

### Trigger

- Roll or choose this encounter when the party needs a break from a high-pressure or stressful section of the session.
    
- Use it to provide a moment of warmth without removing the underlying inequalities and tensions of Arbor.
    

### Initial State

- **Awareness:** Jules notices the party first, though he mistakes one character for a grandchild. Drusilla follows his lead but is more observant than she initially appears.
    
- **Disposition:** Friendly.
    
- **Starting action:** The Pankhursts approach the party at a busy crossroads and insist that one character is their grandchild.
    

Choose one character at random to be the supposed grandchild. If a character has previously expressed a particular vulnerability around family or parental affection, consider selecting them instead.

### Terrain and Conditions

- **Features:** A busy cobblestone crossroads, surrounded by well-maintained streets and affluent residences.
    
- **Cover / elevation / exits:** Numerous side streets and a dense crowd provide opportunities to leave the scene or disappear into the surrounding streets.
    
- **Hazards:** The primary danger is attracting attention by frightening the Pankhursts or causing a public disturbance.
    
- **Light / weather / visibility:** Ordinary Canopy conditions. The crowd makes it difficult to see far down the surrounding streets.
    

---

## Battle Tactics

### Opening Move

- This is a social encounter. Jules and Drusilla approach the party openly and affectionately.
    
- If the party bolts and leaves the couple distressed, increase the local Alarm level by 1.
    

### Priorities

- Jules wants to spend time with his supposed grandchild and take them for ice cream.
    
- Drusilla wants to keep Jules happy and ensure that neither of them comes to harm.
    
- Gil wants to protect the Pankhursts while continuing his business and supporting the Nest.
    

### Morale and Retreat

- **Breaking point:** Gil intervenes if he believes the party is exploiting or endangering the Pankhursts.
    
- **Retreat route:** The party can leave through the surrounding streets. Gil may provide a discreet route out if he trusts them.
    
- **Surrender / parley terms:** There is no planned combat. If the situation escalates, Gil prioritises getting the Pankhursts to safety and will seek help rather than risk their lives.
    

### Reinforcements and Escalation

- **After [X] rounds / on a loud failure:** If the party causes a public disturbance, nearby bystanders may intervene or summon assistance.
    
- **If the party is losing:** Gil attempts to remove the Pankhursts from danger. A Silver Cloak may intervene if the disturbance becomes serious.
    
- **If the party wins quickly:** Not applicable unless the encounter escalates into combat. Otherwise, allow the conversation to resolve naturally.

### Jules Lucas Pankhurst

An elderly gentleman whose face has become so deeply lined that it is difficult to identify his original species. He may once have been human, Beastkin, goblinoid, or one of the Touched.

Jules is kind, generous, and mostly blind. His hearing is poor, and he often loses track of immediate details, but he remembers people and events from decades ago.

### Drusilla Nettie Pankhurst

An elderly woman whose original species is similarly difficult to determine. She is somewhat less impaired than Jules and has an excellent judge of character.

Drusilla allows Jules to make mistakes when they are harmless, but she will not let him make a mistake that puts either of them in danger. Her apparent confusion should never be taken as proof that she has failed to understand what is happening.

### The Pankhursts

The couple made their fortune refining jewels and gems for use in industry and magic throughout Arbor. Although not members of the [[Old Families]], they possess considerable wealth and influence.

They have supported Beastkin businesses and communities for decades, though they have been unable to stop the seizure of Beastkin territory within the Garden Wall.

### Gil

A friendly ice cream merchant from the Nest who has met the party before, depending on their previous interactions. He has connections to the [[Red Wings]] and is deeply grateful to the Pankhursts for their support.

Gil knows several people in the Canopy and may be able to provide information, a place to lie low, or a discreet way out of the area.

His assistance depends on whether he believes the party has treated the Pankhursts well and shown genuine concern for the Beastkin.

## Social

- **Approach:** The party can play along, politely correct the misunderstanding, attempt to deceive the Pankhursts, or excuse themselves.
    
- **Leverage / offer:** Patience and kindness will reassure the couple. Gil's trust depends partly on the party's previous actions towards the Red Wings and Beastkin communities.
    
- **Useful checks:**
    
    - **Wisdom (Insight):** Recognise that Drusilla is more perceptive than her husband's confusion suggests.
        
    - **Charisma (Persuasion):** Reassure the Pankhursts or gently redirect the conversation.
        
    - **Charisma (Deception):** Maintain the mistaken identity if the party deliberately plays along.
        
- **Information gained:** The party may learn about the Pankhursts' charitable work, their support for Beastkin communities, Gil's presence in the Canopy, or local developments relevant to their current objectives.
    

Do not require a check for ordinary kindness or a reasonable explanation. A failed check should complicate the interaction rather than automatically make the Pankhursts hostile.

## Complications

- Roll or choose one if the encounter needs a turn:
    
    - **A Silver Cloak arrives to buy ice cream.** They recognise the Pankhursts and greet them warmly. Their presence may make the party nervous if they are trying to avoid official attention.
        
    - **An unusual magical signature.** A member of the [[Collegiate Arcanum]] detects magic in the area. The source is a mephit hiding beneath the ice cream cart.
        
    - **A familiar face at the funeral.** A member of the [[Order of the Fallen Leaves]] appears to recognise one of the party from a funeral or last rites they attended. They do not necessarily confront the character, but their attention lingers.
        

These complications are optional. Use them to change the pace when appropriate, not to force an escalation.

---

## Outcomes

### Success

If the party treats the Pankhursts well and earns Gil's trust, he offers what help he can.

- **Information:** Gil shares one useful piece of information about the Canopy.
    
- **Safe place:** Gil can arrange a place to lie low if necessary.
    
- **Escape route:** If required, Gil can arrange a discreet way out of the area.
    
- **Free ice cream:** The Pankhursts insist on treating their supposed grandchildren.
    
- **Faction change:** Red Wings faction standing increases by 1 if the party's conduct genuinely merits their goodwill.
    

### Failure or Escape

If the party frightens, exploits, or endangers the Pankhursts, Gil intervenes.

He may attempt to separate the couple from the party, seek help from a Silver Cloak, or arrange for the Pankhursts to leave safely.

If the party causes a serious public disturbance, the encounter may escalate into a confrontation involving Silver Cloaks or other relevant factions.

Politely correcting the misunderstanding or declining the invitation does not count as failure.

### Consequences

- **Immediate:** Increase the local Alarm level by 1 if the party bolts and leaves the Pankhursts distressed, or otherwise causes a public disturbance.
    
- **Later:** Gil will be less willing to trust or assist the party if they exploit or endanger the couple.
    
- **Faction / clock change:** Red Wings faction standing increases by 1 for genuinely considerate behaviour. If the party deliberately exploits the Pankhursts, Red Wings faction standing may decrease by 1. Apply Silver Cloak consequences according to the established faction rules if the party attracts official attention.

## GM Running Tips

- **Pacing note:** Take your time. The Pankhursts are slow, affectionate, and prone to digressions. Allow the party to decide how much they want to engage. Their confusion should be amusing rather than cruel, and their kindness should feel genuine.
    
- **Sensory detail to reuse:** The sweet smell of ice cream, the clink of spoons against brass tubs, and the chatter of children beneath the colourful parasol.
    
- **One memorable beat:** Gil recognises the party, briefly creating tension. Then the children recognise the Pankhursts and gather around to thank them for their support, turning the moment into a small celebration.
    
- **End on:** The Pankhursts sitting together on a bench, eating ice cream and surrounded by their supposed grandchildren. They wave the party off with affectionate confidence, leaving it unclear whether they ever realised the adventurers were strangers.
