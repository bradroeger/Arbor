## Read-Aloud Text

> As you traverse the well maintained cobblestone streets of the Canopy you find yourself crossing a rather busy part of the city. A cross roads is in front of you and people are coming from every direction. Most Dressed in servants garb though you wouldn't know it from the quality or craftsmanship, everything up here appears to be of a better quality after all. Still as you make your way through the crows you feel a hand on your arm, "My dear what are you doing out of school so early?"

Give the players a moment to decide what to do at this juncture. If they simply turn to look you can continue. If they panic and leave then raise the alert level of the area by 1 as Jules and Drusilla believe someone is kidnapping their grandchild.

The players will encounter Jules and Drusilla. Who have both mistaken them for one of their grandaughters or grandsons. Pick one of the characters at random to be the mistaken one. Jules and Drusilla are quite old and it's even difficult to know what species they truly are at this point as much of their facial features are hidden by wrinkles. Both Jules and Drusilla should not be out alone as they are mostly blind and a bit deaf but are sweet old dears and something of local celebreties at this point. 

The players may take many tacks lying or trying to convince the pair they are mistaken
