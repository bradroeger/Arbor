## Read-Aloud Text

> The sound of merrymaking fills the air, as in front of you a small troupe of performers takes to a stage. The crowd moves enough to allow you to see the show in full.
> As the crowd in front parts you see 6 performers dressed in various costumes. 4 of the performers appear to be monstrous beasts of some kind, their cloth and leather outfits conjuring images of the formless creatures that inhabit the Great Sky Highway. The remaining two cloaked in black coats, weapons at their back and hips stand across from the others. All wear identical white featureless masks that obscure their faces. 
> The crowd holds its breath as the black cloaks draw their blades the sound of metal leaving its sheath unnaturally audible. A black cloak raises the blade in their hand pointing skyward. A moment later they let it fall to point towards the monsters and the two groups charge at each swinging blade and claw wildly.
> The two sides clash. Coloured ribbon erupts as steel and claw strike true. As the ribbon fills the small stage much is obscured until only the ribbon and the occasional glint of steel flash through. 
> The ribbons eventually fall, the crowd is quiet and all the actors are upon the ground. One of the black cloaks slowly begins to stir. A murmur and barely audible whispers of encouragement spread through the crowd. The black cloak continues to slowly move. In a sudden burst of energy the performer springs to his feet in an acrobatic manoeuvre as cheers rise from the crowd.  The performer waves their arms and puts a finger to their lips. 
> They make a great show of looking around the apparent battlefield until they see their fallen comrade. They step towards them picking up the fallen one's arm and watch it fall again and again limply to their side. From the crowd a sharp intake of breath and muttering as the performer shakes their head then shuddering as if weeping. 
> A moment later they slowly begin to rise from the floor but before they are fully standing kneel back down and lift the hand once more. The hand catches the air. A cheer goes up as the performer jumps into the air. The cheering becomes louder and the black cloak runs off stage returning with a wheel barrow and hurls the "injured" person in. 
> As they charge off stage the person pushing falls into the wheelbarrow which carries on rolling its handles floating in the air. The crowd claps and laughs.
