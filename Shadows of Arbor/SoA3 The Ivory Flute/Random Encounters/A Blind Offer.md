## Read-Aloud Text

> _The sound of merry making and mirth fills the air, as in front of you a small troupe of performers. The crowd moves enough to allow you to see the show in full._
> As the crowd in front parts you see 6 performers dressed in various costumes. 4 of the performers appear to be monstrous beasts of some kind, their cloth and leather outfits conjuring images of the formless creatures that inhabit the Great Sky Highway. The remaining two cloaked in black coats, weapons at their back and hips. All wear identical white featureless masks that obscure their faces. The crowd holds it's breath as the black cloaks draw their blades the sound of metal leaving it's sheath unnaturally audible. The hand with the blade is raised and the two groups charge at each swinging blade and claw wildly. 
> The two sides clash and as each finds purchase against their intended target coloured ribbon erupts from wounds imagined. As the ribbon fills the small stage much is obscured until only the ribbon and the occasional glint of steel flashes through. 
> The ribbons eventually fall, the crowd is quite and all the actors are upon the ground. One of the black cloaks slowly stirs, a murmur from the audience and a moment later the performer springs to his feet in an acrobatic maneuverer as cheers rise from the crowd.  The performer waves their arms and puts a finger to their lips. 
> They makes a great show of looking around and sees his fallen comrade. They step towards them picking up their arm and watching it fall again and again limpy to their side. From the crowd a sharp intake of breath and muttering as the performer tries one last time, their hand catches the air. A cheer goes up as the performer runs off stage and returns with a wheel barrow hurls the "injured" person into their wheel barrow and charge off stage falling into the wheel barrow which carries on rolling it's handles floating in the air. The crowd claps and laughs.
